\section{Symbol dictionary}
\label{app:symbols}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.22\linewidth} p{0.72\linewidth}}
$\Omega\subset\R^3$ & Intrinsic/material domain of the brane (coordinates $x=(x^1,x^2,x^3)$).\\
$\vecX(x,t)\in\R^4$ & Embedding field of the brane into ambient 4D Euclidean space.\\
$t$ & External evolution parameter (time).\\
$g_{ij}$ & Induced metric: $g_{ij}=\partial_i\vecX\cdot\partial_j\vecX$.\\
$g^0_{ij}$ & Isotropic reference metric: $g^0_{ij}=\ell_0^2\,\delta_{ij}$ (stress-free scale $\ell_0$).\\
$h_\star$ & Held ground-state scale (e.g. imposed by boundary conditions).\\
$\alpha$ & Dimensionless pre-stress parameter: $\alpha=\ell_0/h_\star\in(0,1]$.\\
$\widehat g$ & Mixed relative metric: $\widehat g=(g^0)^{-1}g$.\\
$E$ & Green--Lagrange strain: $E=\tfrac12(\widehat g-I)$.\\
$\lambda,\mu$ & Lam\'e parameters of the StVK baseline stored energy.\\
$\rho_m$ & Brane mass density in the Lagrangian.\\
$W(g;g^0)$ & Isotropic stored-energy density (hyperelastic).\\
$P_A^{\;i}$ & First Piola stress: $P_A^{\;i}=\partial W/\partial(\partial_i X^A)$.\\
$\vecu$ & Displacement field relative to a background: $\vecu=\vecX-\vecX_\star$.\\
$u$ & Shorthand for a transverse component in a graph-type embedding, $\vecX\approx(x,u)$.\\
$\bm{\epsilon}$ & Normalized polarization state of a narrowband multi-component wave.\\
$a_\mu$ & Berry (rank-1) connection: $a_\mu=\ii\langle u\,|\,\partial_\mu u\rangle$.\\
$f_{\mu\nu}$ & Berry curvature: $f_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu a_\nu-\partial_\nu a_\mu$.\\
$\mathcal{A}_\mu$ & Wilczek--Zee connection on an $n$-dimensional polarization subspace.\\
$\mathcal{F}_{\mu\nu}$ & Wilczek--Zee curvature: $\partial_\mu\mathcal{A}_\nu-\partial_\nu\mathcal{A}_\mu-\ii[\mathcal{A}_\mu,\mathcal{A}_\nu]$.\\
$\gamma_\Gamma$ & Geometric phase / holonomy around loop $\Gamma$: $\gamma_\Gamma=\oint_\Gamma a_\mu\,\dd x^\mu$.\\
$\Omega_{\mathrm{mix}}$ & Mode-mixing (non-adiabatic conversion) rate between nearby polarization bands.\\
$\Delta\omega_{\mathrm{gap}}$ & Local spectral gap to the nearest competing polarization band.\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

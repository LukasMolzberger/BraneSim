\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We formulated a rotationally invariant continuum substrate model for a tensioned 3D elastic medium embedded in $\R^4$,
and proposed a concrete diagnostic pipeline to test whether geometric phases of polarized substrate waves can support
an effective gauge interpretation.
We also outlined how particle-like localized modes can be posed as self-adjoint eigenproblems around symmetric backgrounds,
and how geometric nonlinearity can plausibly provide self-confinement without imposing ad-hoc constraints.

A key structural choice is the separation of conceptual claims (continuum model, emergence map, falsifiable diagnostics)
from discrete numerical implementation details (moved to an appendix).
The next milestones are therefore unambiguous: demonstrate robust geometric-phase holonomy behavior and
stable localized modes in parameter regimes where the continuum assumptions are well-controlled.

# What critics say, and whether it still bites

1. **Electron is experimentally pointlike at extremely small scales**

* **Evidence**: High-energy scattering and contact-interaction searches find no electron substructure down to scales ≲10⁻¹⁹–10⁻²⁰ m; analyses translate null deviations into multi-TeV contact-scale bounds, i.e., no form-factor roll-off visible. ([Physical Review Link Manager][1])
* **Why it’s a problem**: W&vdM’s natural size is O(λ_C) ≈2.4×10⁻¹² m (though they argue “apparent” size shrinks with γ and interactions look pointlike). ([fondationlouisdebroglie.org][2])
* **Under your assumptions**: If your nonlinear medium enforces that all four-momentum is carried by a single confined EM mode whose **effective** interaction region Lorentz-contracts and scales with de Broglie wavelength (W&vdM’s argument), the phenomenology can remain pointlike in hard scattering. But you still need a **quantitative** prediction: compute scattering form factors and show they track the SM pointlike cross sections within experimental errors across Q². **Status**: *Partially addressed in spirit, not yet demonstrated.*

2. **Spin–statistics and fermionic degrees of freedom**

* **Evidence**: In QFT, spin-½ fermions require anticommuting fields; bosonic (spin-1) photons cannot just “reconfigure” into fermions without introducing the right operator content and statistics. That’s the spin–statistics theorem’s domain. ([Wikipedia][3])
* **Why it’s a problem**: W&vdM is semiclassical; it sketches SU(2)-like behavior from topology (double loop, 720°), but doesn’t supply a fermionic field/anticommutator structure. ([fondationlouisdebroglie.org][2])
* **Under your assumptions**: If your ontology includes a **nonlinear EM-like field** with additional internal/topological sectors that *quantize* into effective fermionic excitations, you must still recover antisymmetric many-electron states and Pauli exclusion in full. That needs a field-level construction (algebra + microcausality) beyond semiclassical pictures. **Status**: *Still problematic until a proper fermionic field realization is given.*

3. **Magnetic moment (g−2) precision**

* **Evidence**: The SM (QED + weak + hadronic) matches the electron’s magnetic moment to ~1 part in 10¹²; most recent Penning-trap results and theory agree exquisitely. Any alternative must match this level to be viable. ([Physical Review Link Manager][4])
* **Why it’s a problem**: W&vdM reproduces the **Schwinger term** (~α/2π) qualitatively via energy inside/outside the torus, but offers no systematic path to higher loops and tiny residuals that experiments see. ([fondationlouisdebroglie.org][2])
* **Under your assumptions**: If your theory reduces to QED’s renormalized predictions in the appropriate limit, you can inherit the precision. If it **replaces** QED, you need a new perturbation scheme over your nonlinear medium that reproduces the full (g−2) series to 10⁻¹². **Status**: *Unaddressed at required precision (critical).*

4. **Charge quantization and Gauss’s law**

* **Evidence**: Charge universality and exactness across processes are nailed down experimentally; QED enforces it via gauge symmetry. ([Wikipedia][5])
* **Why it’s a problem**: W&vdM estimates |q|≈0.91e from a geometric average field near r≈λ_C/4π; this is a **phenomenological estimate**, not an exact quantization mechanism from gauge invariance. ([fondationlouisdebroglie.org][2])
* **Under your assumptions**: If “topological charge” in your medium **is** the U(1) gauge charge and exactly quantized by a topological invariant, spell out the invariant and why it enforces q=e identically (not ~0.91e). **Status**: *Open—needs exact topological quantization argument.*

5. **Confinement / stability mechanism**

* **Evidence**: W&vdM appeals to self-confinement in a “curved space” / nonlinear vacuum; cites soliton analogies and special EM configurations but no explicit, stable, dynamical solution with Maxwell-like equations plus nonlinearity. ([fondationlouisdebroglie.org][2])
* **Under your assumptions**: You postulate a nonlinear threshold medium. Good—now provide the **equations**, conserved quantities, and a **localized, stable** solution carrying charge, spin-½, and the right dispersion. Ideally, demonstrate **numerical** stability and spectra. **Status**: *Plausible within your framework, but still to be constructed.*

6. **Compatibility with standard processes (Breit–Wheeler, scattering, atomic QED)**

* **Evidence**: Pair production γγ→e⁺e⁻ has firm QED predictions; experimental programs to see the linear Breit–Wheeler with real photons are active and consistent with QED modeling. Bound-state QED (hydrogenic spectra, Lamb shift, etc.) is an enormous success. ([cds.cern.ch][6])
* **Under your assumptions**: Your model should reduce to QED cross-sections for these processes or explain tiny, energy-dependent deviations that are **within** current experimental bounds. **Status**: *Needs quantitative matching to known cross-sections and atomic QED.*

7. **Lepton universality & weak interactions**

* **Evidence**: Electrons, muons, and taus fit into SU(2)×U(1) electroweak doublets with well-tested couplings.
* **Why it’s a problem**: If “electron = confined photon,” what are muon and tau? W&vdM speculates different curvature/loops; but you also need the **correct weak charges** and universality and neutrino couplings. ([fondationlouisdebroglie.org][2])
* **Under your assumptions**: You’ll need a mapping from your topological sectors to the full SM gauge representation content. **Status**: *Unaddressed.*

# If we keep W&vdM‐style intuition inside your model, what to do next (concrete, falsifiable steps)

4. **(g−2)**: Either (a) demonstrate your theory **reduces to QED** for perturbative radiative corrections, or (b) re-derive the full series within your framework and match the latest number. ([Physical Review Link Manager][4])

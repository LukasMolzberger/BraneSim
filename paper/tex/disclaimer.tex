\section{Disclaimer and Methodological
Approach}\label{disclaimer-and-methodological-approach}

This paper presents a conceptual and philosophical exploration of a 3D
brane-based model of quantum mechanics and gravitation. The author does
not claim formal academic training in physics; instead, the theory arose
through a non-traditional process dubbed \emph{ai coding}---an
intuitive blend of pattern recognition, conceptual synthesis, and
interactive engagement with large language models (LLMs).

\paragraph{Methodological Approach}

Informal resources such as YouTube played a key role by providing
accessible explanations and visual insights that helped shape a broad
understanding. While these sources have clear limitations, this approach
enabled rapid cross-disciplinary learning, avoided narrow technical
silos, and encouraged novel connections between ideas that might not emerge through traditional training pathways.

The goal is not to present a fully validated theory, but to offer a
coherent alternative interpretation of quantum mechanics and relativity
grounded in the concept of an underlying brane medium. Readers are invited to
treat this as a speculative proposal and to engage with it critically
and openly.

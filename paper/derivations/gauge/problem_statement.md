# Gauge Bridge — Problem Statement

The gauge coupling structure is already established: Berry connection a_μ = i⟨u|∂_μu⟩ is formally identical to A_μ, Berry curvature f_μν is formally identical to F_μν, and the matter covariant derivative (∂_μ − ia_μ)Ψ enters the envelope equation. The remaining open derivation is: show that free fluctuations of the Berry connection propagate as massless modes with Maxwell (or Yang-Mills) dispersion.

## Specific tasks

**(a) Effective action for a_μ.** Derive the effective action for slow variations of a_μ by integrating out fast substrate modes. The starting point is the full substrate action S[R]; integrate over the short-wavelength (UV) modes at one loop to obtain S_eff[a_μ].

**(b) Maxwell form.** Show the resulting effective Lagrangian takes the Maxwell form −¼F_μν F^μν in the appropriate (long-wavelength, adiabatic) limit. The key question is whether the coefficient of the kinetic term is positive and finite (not zero, not infinite).

**(c) Photon propagation speed.** Show the propagation speed of gauge fluctuations matches the substrate's characteristic speed c. This requires that the effective action produces massless dispersion ω = c|k| for the gauge mode, not a massive or subluminal spectrum.

# Lorentz Bridge — Problem Statement

Derive the dual-observer argument rigorously: show that if both the medium AND the inside observers built from it are governed by the same anisotropic stiffness tensor, the effective metric experienced by inside observers is isotropic even when the lab metric is not.

## Specific quantitative tasks

**(a) Rod/clock/signal renormalization.** Derive the renormalization of rods, clocks, and signals built from the same medium branch. Show that an inside observer constructed from the same elastic modes as the medium measures an isotropic speed of light even though the lab dispersion relation has a 7.5% anisotropy at α=0.2.

**(b) Residual birefringence bound.** Give a quantitative upper bound on residual birefringence as a function of (k·a) and α. The leading-order anisotropy is zeroth-order in ka (not O((ka)²)), so the bound must come from the inside-observer universality argument, not from the long-wavelength limit alone.

**(c) SME parameter mapping.** Map the residual anisotropy to experimental Lorentz-violation bounds (Standard Model Extension parameters). At α=0.2 the L–T speed ratio is √(1−α) ≈ 0.894; internal-observer cancellation must reduce this to within current SME sensitivity (c-coefficients < O(10⁻¹⁵)).

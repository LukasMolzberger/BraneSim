\section{Interfaces}
\label{sec:interfaces}

This paper imports the following results from Papers~I and~III without re-derivation, and
exports the localized-mode results consumed by Paper~V.

\subsection*{From Paper~I}
\begin{description}
  \item[\normalfont Equations of motion.] \Cref{eq:eom}.
  \item[\normalfont Geometric nonlinearity and quartic coefficient.]
  The constitutive quartic $W_4\propto k_s\alpha/a$ (\Cref{eq:W4}), which provides the
  anti-expansion stabilization of any localized mode.
  \item[\normalfont Induced metric and near-identity expansion.]
  \Cref{eq:induced-metric,eq:metric-near-identity}; the transverse--lateral coupling
  $\partial_i u\,\partial_j u$ underlies both the gravity channel (Paper~II) and the
  geometric self-guidance discussed here.
  \item[\normalfont Continuum action.] \Cref{eq:action}.
  \item[\normalfont Discrete lattice action.] \Cref{eq:discrete-4d-action};
  the lattice spacing $a$ is the UV cutoff that blocks Derrick collapse.
  \item[\normalfont Bell interpretive stance.] \Cref{sec:bell-interpretation} of Paper~I.
\end{description}

\subsection*{From Paper~III}
\begin{description}
  \item[\normalfont $U(3)$ triplet decomposition.]
  The axis-aligned lateral triplet $\Psi\in\C^3$, its $\mathfrak{u}(3)=\mathfrak{u}(1)
  \oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)$ decomposition, and the $U(1)$ trace identification with
  electromagnetism (Paper~III \Cref{eq:u3-decomp,eq:em-trace}).
  \item[\normalfont Kinematic color confinement.]
  The result that color charge cannot be carried to infinity by a free linear excitation
  (Paper~III \Cref{subsec:color-confinement}).
  \item[\normalfont Charge-triality locking.]
  $q\equiv -t\pmod{3}$, which constrains the soliton labels defined in
  \Cref{subsec:soliton-labels}.
  \item[\normalfont Gauge-covariant envelope dynamics.]
  The covariant derivative $\mathcal{D}_\mu\Psi = (\partial_\mu + \ii\mathcal{A}_\mu)\Psi$
  and effective action $S_{\text{eff}}$ (Paper~III \Cref{eq:cov-deriv,eq:Seff}).
\end{description}

\subsection*{Exported to Paper~V}
\begin{description}
  \item[\normalfont Localized soliton modes and chirality.]
  The localized matter modes (\Cref{sec:matter}) and their forward/backward propagation along
  the timelike axis, which Paper~V (Bell/interpretation) reads as matter and antimatter
  worldtubes.
\end{description}

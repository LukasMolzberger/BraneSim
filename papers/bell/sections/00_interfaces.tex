\section{Imported interfaces from Paper~I}
\label{sec:interfaces}

This paper imports the following results from Paper~I without re-derivation.

\begin{description}
  \item[\normalfont Substrate ontology.]
  The 4D cubic brane lattice embedded codimension-zero in a 4D Euclidean ambient, with the
  timelike direction selected by the Lorentzian sign structure of the brane action
  (\Cref{subsec:ontology-kinematics}).
  The substrate is manifestly local: all forces propagate through direct spring-neighbor contact
  at finite speed.

  \item[\normalfont Time-symmetry of the brane action.]
  The action (\Cref{eq:action}) is time-symmetric: it selects a \emph{kind} of direction
  (timelike vs spacelike) but not a \emph{direction} along the timelike axis
  (past-to-future vs future-to-past).
  The arrow of time is therefore not a property of the substrate.

  \item[\normalfont Soliton chirality.]
  The concept that localized modes (Paper~IV \Cref{sec:matter}) can propagate forward or backward
  along the brane's timelike axis, selecting the causal direction at the soliton level rather than
  at the substrate level.

  \item[\normalfont Determinism.]
  The substrate equations of motion (\Cref{eq:eom}) are deterministic: given a complete
  initial configuration, all future and past configurations are uniquely fixed.
\end{description}

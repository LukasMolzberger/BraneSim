% Abstract and keywords
\abstract{We present the ontological foundation and shared mathematical structure for a series of papers investigating a minimal deterministic substrate hypothesis. The substrate is a 4D cubic lattice embedded with codimension zero in a 4D Euclidean ambient; the timelike direction is selected by the Lorentzian sign structure of the brane action, not by the ambient geometry. Its essential nonlinearity is purely geometric: the induced metric contains the coupling $\partial_i u\,\partial_j u$ when the embedding is excited transversely, producing lateral contraction without any auxiliary field. We derive the continuum hyperelastic action from the discrete lattice energy, establish the equations of motion, and characterize the linearized wave-branch structure, including the transverse and longitudinal speeds $c_T^2=(1-\alpha)k_s a^2/m$ and $c_L^2=k_s a^2/m$. The transverse--lateral coupling is the shared geometric origin of Lorentz, gauge, color, gravity, matter, and mass bridges developed in companion papers; the present paper exports the interface equations each companion imports. We also commit to a retrocausal worldtube interpretation forced by Bell's theorem under the assumptions of determinism, measurement independence, and a finite-speed substrate: entanglement is the continuity of a branching 4D worldtube, and the arrow of causality is a property of soliton chirality.}

\keyword{deterministic substrate; brane lattice; geometric nonlinearity; induced metric; hyperelastic action; linearized waves; Bell's theorem; retrocausal worldtube}

\section{Quantitative $U(1)/SU(3)$ sector split}
\label{sec:split}

The rank selection and the relative strength of the two sectors are controlled by the prestress
parameters. Analytically, the vacuum branch stiffnesses~\eqref{eq:speeds} give two candidate
near-degenerate triplets with isolation ratios
\begin{equation}
  \text{(amplitude split)}\ \frac{(1-\alpha_s)\alpha_t}{\alpha_s},
  \qquad
  \text{(longitudinal split)}\ \frac{\alpha_s}{(1-\alpha_s)\alpha_t},
\end{equation}
in both of which $\gamma_t$ \emph{cancels}: $\gamma_t$ sets only the overall energy/light-cone
scale, while the sector split is governed by $(\alpha_s,\alpha_t)$ alone.

Computed on the nonlinear carrier $D_{\bar R}(\veck)$ \citep{repo}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Rank selection $U(3)$, not $U(4)$.} The best rank-$n$ near-degenerate cluster
  isolation (gap/spread, $k$-averaged) is $18.9,\ 5.1,\ 2.4,\ 0.7$ for $n=2,3,4,5$. The rank-3
  triplet is well isolated while any rank-4 cluster is marginal: the transported carrier is
  $U(3)$, and $U(4)/SU(4)$ is spectrally excluded---quantitatively, not by fiat.
  \item \textbf{$\lambda_4$ separation opened by $\alpha_t$.} The rank-3 isolation grows toward
  $\alpha_t\to 1$, matching the analytic amplitude-split scaling; $\alpha_t$ (with $\gamma_t$)
  opens the gap that splits the fourth mode off the triplet.
  \item \textbf{$\su{3}$ generic, $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ measure-zero.} The robust Lie rank is $8$
  across the entire $(\alpha_s,\alpha_t)$ plane sampled; isolated $3$'s are $k^\ast$-specific
  accidents, consistent with the genericity of Section~\ref{subsec:generic}.
  \item \textbf{Coupling split.} The abelian and non-abelian kinetic stiffnesses satisfy
  $1/e^2\approx 1.6$ and $1/g^2\approx 12$, i.e.\ the $SU(3)$ sector carries $\sim 8\times$ the
  total stiffness (eight generators to the $U(1)$'s one) but is roughly \emph{equipartitioned
  per generator} ($1/g^2/8\approx 1.5\approx 1/e^2$), and the ratio is stable ($\approx
  0.13$--$0.15$) across the parameters.
\end{itemize}

The overall picture: $\alpha_s$ controls spatial branch mixing and the longitudinal--transverse
gap (and is required nonzero for the gauge sector and the lab anisotropy of
Section~\ref{sec:metric}); $\alpha_t$ opens the $\lambda_4$ gap that enforces $U(3)$-not-$U(4)$;
and $\gamma_t$ only rescales. A natural, more predictive special case is $\alpha_t=\alpha_s$
(single prestress dial), which ties the sectors and the gravity channel to one number; the
present computations use independent $(\alpha_s,\alpha_t)$ because $\gamma_t$ cancels from the
split, and we flag the single-dial option as a falsifiable structural choice for future work.

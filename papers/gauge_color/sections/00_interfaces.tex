\section{Imported interfaces from Paper~I}
\label{sec:interfaces}

This paper imports the following results from Paper~I without re-derivation.
All equation and section labels without an explicit paper prefix refer to Paper~I.

\begin{description}
  \item[\normalfont Substrate model and equations of motion.]
  The 4D brane embedding, induced metric $g_{ij}$ (\Cref{eq:induced-metric}), constitutive law
  (\Cref{subsec:constitutive-law}), and equations of motion (\Cref{eq:eom}).

  \item[\normalfont Linearized branch structure.]
  The long-wavelength dispersion $\omega_b^2(\veck)\approx c_b^2|\veck|^2$
  (\Cref{eq:linear-dispersion}), branch speeds $c_T^2=(1-\alpha)k_s a^2/m$ and
  $c_L^2=k_s a^2/m$ (\Cref{eq:wave-speeds-derived}).

  \item[\normalfont Axis-aligned triplet structure.]
  The three spacelike embedding components $(X^1,X^2,X^3)$ on the 6-neighbor cubic lattice
  define an axis-aligned triplet $\Psi=(\psi_x,\psi_y,\psi_z)^\top\in\C^3$, with
  $\alpha$-driven degeneracy described in \Cref{sec:waves} of Paper~I.

  \item[\normalfont Geometric nonlinearity.]
  The constitutive quartic coefficient $\propto k_s\alpha/a$ from \Cref{eq:W4}, which sets
  the energetic coupling between the $U(1)$ and $SU(3)$ sectors at finite amplitude.

  \item[\normalfont Bell interpretive stance.]
  Retrocausal worldtube reading from \Cref{sec:bell-interpretation} of Paper~I, inherited as
  a fixed commitment.
\end{description}

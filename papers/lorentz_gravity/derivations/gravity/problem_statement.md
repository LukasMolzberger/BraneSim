# Gravity Bridge — Problem Statement

Derive the Newtonian limit from the transverse-excitation contraction channel.

## Specific tasks

**(a) Poisson-type equation.** Show that a slowly varying transverse excitation u(x,t) sources a slowly varying strain field ξ(x,t) obeying a Poisson-type equation:

    ∇²ξ = −4πG_eff ρ_eff

where ρ_eff is proportional to the local energy density of the excitation. The mechanism: the induced metric g_ij = h★² δ_ij + ∂_i u ∂_j u has a transverse-gradient term ∂_i u ∂_j u that, under prestretch (α > 0), produces inward lateral response — the contraction channel.

**(b) Probe bending.** Show that the slow strain field ξ(x,t) bends the propagation of probe wavepackets in the manner of a weak gravitational potential. Specifically: derive the geodesic deviation equation for a probe wavepacket in the background strain field, and show that it matches the Newtonian equation of motion ẍ = −∇Φ_grav with Φ_grav ∝ ξ.

**(c) Effective coupling.** Derive the effective coupling constant G_eff in terms of substrate parameters (k_s, α, a, ρ_m) from the spatial contraction channel alone. G_eff is expected to scale with α (the spatial prestress that governs the quartic self-confinement), co-varying with binding strength κ_bind (`papers/core/derivations/status.md` A4a): ∂ ln G_eff / ∂ ln α = ∂ ln κ_bind / ∂ ln α.

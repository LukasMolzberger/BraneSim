\section{Imported interfaces from Paper~I}
\label{sec:interfaces}

This paper imports the following results from Paper~I without re-derivation.
All equation labels refer to Paper~I unless otherwise indicated.
This paper has no downstream dependents in the series and exports no interfaces.

\begin{description}
  \item[\normalfont Substrate model.]
  The 4D cubic brane lattice, its embedding map $\vecX:\Omega\times\R\to\R^4$, the discrete
  lattice energy (\Cref{eq:discrete-4d-action}), and its continuum limit (\Cref{eq:action}).

  \item[\normalfont Induced metric.]
  $g_{ij} = \partial_i\vecX\cdot\partial_j\vecX$ (\Cref{eq:induced-metric}), and its
  near-identity expansion around a homogeneous background (\Cref{eq:metric-near-identity}):
  \begin{equation}
    g_{ij} = h_\star^2\delta_{ij}
    + h_\star(\partial_i\xi_j+\partial_j\xi_i)
    + \partial_i\vec\xi\cdot\partial_j\vec\xi
    + \partial_i u\,\partial_j u.
    \tag{P1:\ref{eq:metric-near-identity}}
  \end{equation}

  \item[\normalfont Equations of motion.]
  \Cref{eq:eom} of Paper~I.

  \item[\normalfont Linearized branch speeds.]
  $c_T^2=(1-\alpha)\,k_s a^2/m$ and $c_L^2=k_s a^2/m$ from \Cref{eq:wave-speeds-derived}.

  \item[\normalfont Long-wavelength dispersion.]
  $\omega_b^2(\veck)\approx c_b^2\,|\veck|^2$ for $|\veck|\to 0$ (\Cref{eq:linear-dispersion}).

  \item[\normalfont Quasi-static in-brane equilibrium and contraction channel.]
  \Cref{subsec:gravity-channel} of Paper~I, which derives the in-brane forcing problem sourced
  by $\partial_i u\,\partial_j u$.

  \item[\normalfont Bell interpretive stance.]
  The retrocausal worldtube reading from \Cref{sec:bell-interpretation} of Paper~I, imported as
  a fixed interpretive commitment.
\end{description}

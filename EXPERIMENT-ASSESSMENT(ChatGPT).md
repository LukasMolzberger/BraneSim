## How I would upgrade the solver and the diagnostics

On the solver side, I would stop spending money on larger runs of the **same unconstrained problem**. The literature standard here is clear: once one nonlinear branch has been found repeatedly, the next step is **branch-selection machinery**, not more of the same Newton basin. That means a bordered solve with **phase conditions** to remove the translational, global-phase, and time-shift neutral modes; **pseudo-arclength continuation** in a physically meaningful parameter such as `ω`, `Q`, or your effective `β`; and **deflation** once the known unwanted branch has been found, so the next solve is forced away from it. Continuation packages do this by adding auxiliary phase equations; deflation methods were developed exactly to escape repeated convergence to the same solution. citeturn9academia0turn9academia2turn7academia0

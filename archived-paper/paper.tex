% Abstract and keywords
\abstract{We formulate a minimal deterministic substrate hypothesis and examine whether the main structures required of a viable physical ontology---effective Lorentz symmetry, gauge-like transport, localized matter, a gravity-like coupling, and a Bell-compatible interpretation---can be consistently connected within a single coherent framework. The paper's contribution is not a finished alternative to established theories but a substrate-consistency argument: by making each connection explicit and mathematically grounded, a broad ontological claim is transformed into a constrained research program with concrete failure points. The substrate is a 4D cubic brane lattice embedded with codimension zero in a 4D Euclidean ambient; the timelike direction is selected by the Lorentzian sign structure of the brane action rather than by the ambient geometry. The long-wavelength effective description on a spacelike slice uses an isotropic hyperelastic action built from the induced metric, with a homogeneous mismatch parameter $\alpha$ encoding the prestretch. A central commitment is that the essential nonlinearity is geometric: the 4D Euclidean link length and induced metric generate nonlinear amplitude--lateral coupling even when the microscopic spring law is Hooke-linear. Five consistency bridges are developed from this mechanism: (i)~\emph{Lorentz}---polarized wave branches yield effective Lorentz-type kinematics for inside observers; (ii)~\emph{Gauge}---adiabatic transport of each band-isolated wavepacket's polarization eigenbundle induces Berry or Wilczek--Zee connections, the natural gauge variables of the per-wavepacket envelope theory; (iii)~\emph{Color}---an axis-aligned triplet on the cubic substrate supports a $U(3)$ transport problem with $SU(3)$-like traceless structure; (iv)~\emph{Gravity}---localized transverse excitation produces an inward lateral contraction field via the induced-metric coupling; (v)~\emph{Bell}---causality is assigned to soliton chirality and entanglement is the continuity of a branching 4D worldtube, a retrocausal Bell-compatible completion. The decisive first numerical test is the existence or failure of stable, baryon-like confined triplet modes on the cubic substrate.}

\keyword{deterministic substrate; brane lattice; geometric nonlinearity; emergent relativity; Berry phase; gauge emergence; soliton; Bell's theorem}

# Color Bridge — Problem Statement

Show that the axis-aligned triplet U(3) structure gives rise to genuine color-like dynamics. The kinematic confinement argument (no free colored asymptotic states because coherence and color-activity are mutually exclusive on the cubic lattice) is established.

## Remaining tasks

**(a) Falsifiable holonomy ratio.** Show that the holonomy ratio R(α₂)/R(α₁) = [(3−2α₂)/α₂] / [(3−2α₁)/α₁] is a falsifiable prediction distinguishing this model. Procedure: off-[111] (e.g. [100], k₀a = π/4) build band-isolated ℂ³ envelopes at α = 0.2 and α = 0.5; transport each around a fixed small loop γ in (x,t); compute 𝒜_μ = i⟨Ψ|∂_μΨ⟩, split by P_U(1), P_SU(3); form R(α); compare R(0.5)/R(0.2) to 0.3077.

**(b) U(3) to SU(3) identification.** Clarify the relationship between U(3) triplet transport and SU(3) in the Standard Model. The algebraic decomposition U(3) = U(1) × SU(3) / ℤ₃ is correct, but the physical identification of the traceless sector with QCD color is not yet justified. Specifically: show that the three axis-aligned directions {ê_x, ê_y, ê_z} on the cubic lattice map to the fundamental representation of SU(3), and that the color-charge assignments are consistent with quark quantum numbers.

**(c) Stiefel-Whitney topology.** Address the topology issue: real bundles can have nontrivial Stiefel-Whitney classes even when Berry curvature vanishes. The statement that real symmetric D(k) implies trivial plaquette holonomy must be qualified: it implies trivial first Chern class, but not trivial w₁ or w₂. Determine whether the substrate's real bundle has nontrivial Stiefel-Whitney topology, and what physical consequence this has for color confinement.

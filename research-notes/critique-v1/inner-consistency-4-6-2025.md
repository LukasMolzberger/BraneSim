## 🔍 Identified Issues and Potential Inconsistencies

### **1. Time as Fifth Dimension vs. Determinism and Causality**

* **Claim:** “Time is treated as a fifth dimension, through which changes propagate.”
* **Tension:** You describe a deterministic theory where wave packets evolve via local interactions and causally propagate through neighboring points — this suggests **time as a parameter**, not a true geometric dimension.
* **Issue:** In relativity, time is one of the four dimensions; adding it as a fifth requires justification. If the fourth spatial dimension is amplitudal and not part of spacetime, why is time outside the 3+1? This clashes with your claim of emergent Lorentz invariance.
* **Suggested Clarification:** Explicitly define whether “time” is a **physical dimension** in the same sense as space, or an **evolution parameter** in the wave equation (like in Newtonian or Hamiltonian mechanics).

### **2. Local Coupling vs. Emergent Nonlocal Effects**

## 🔍 The *Freedom-of-Choice* Loophole: What It Is

Bell's theorem assumes that:

* The **settings** of measurement devices (e.g., angles of polarizers) are **statistically independent** of the hidden variables that determine the outcomes.

Formally, Bell assumes:

$$
P(\lambda \mid a, b) = P(\lambda)
$$

where:

* $\lambda$ = hidden variables
* $a, b$ = measurement settings

This means: **the settings are chosen freely**, not influenced by or correlated with the internal state of the system being measured.

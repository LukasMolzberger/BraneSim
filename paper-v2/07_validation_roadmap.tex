\section{Validation roadmap and falsifiable checks}
\label{sec:validation}

This section lists validation targets that do not depend on any particular discretization.
Implementation details for one reference lattice realization are moved to \Cref{app:discrete}.

\subsection{Continuum-level checks}

\paragraph{Rotational invariance.}
Because $W$ depends only on invariants of $g$ relative to $\bar g$,
the continuum formulation is rotationally invariant by construction.
A practical check is that linearized dispersion relations and wave speeds are isotropic.

\paragraph{Linear regime and dispersion.}
Extract the small-amplitude dispersion relation for each branch
(transverse/longitudinal or more general polarizations) and verify the existence of a stable,
approximately linear wave cone over a range of wavelengths.

\paragraph{Energy conservation.}
For time-independent $W$, the Hamiltonian
\begin{equation}
  H = \int_\Omega \left(\frac{\rho_m}{2}|\partial_t \vecX|^2 + W(g;\bar g)\right)\mathrm{d}^3x
\end{equation}
should be conserved for solutions of \eqref{eq:eom}, up to boundary fluxes.

\subsection{Geometric-phase diagnostics}

\paragraph{Gauge invariance.}
Compute $A_\mu$ and $F_{\mu\nu}$ from a polarized wave state and confirm invariance of $F$ under
$\bm{\epsilon}\mapsto e^{i\chi}\bm{\epsilon}$.

\paragraph{Loop holonomy.}
Measure $\Phi_\gamma=\oint_\gamma A$ for families of loops and verify the Stokes-type relation
\begin{equation}
  \oint_{\partial\Sigma} A \approx \int_\Sigma F
\end{equation}
in regimes where $\bm{\epsilon}$ varies smoothly.

\paragraph{Propagation behavior.}
Test whether localized disturbances in $F_{\mu\nu}$ propagate with a consistent characteristic speed,
and whether sourceless regions exhibit approximately divergence-free behavior (in a chosen effective gauge).

\subsection{Localized mode checks}

\paragraph{Self-adjoint spectrum.}
For a fixed, numerically constructed background $\vecX_0$, discretize the linearized operator and test:
real eigenvalues, orthogonality of modes, and robustness under domain size increase.

\paragraph{Self-confinement.}
Initialize candidate localized states and test persistence, energy localization measures, and
radiation loss over long times. Identify parameter regimes where confinement is metastable or stable.

\subsection{Failure modes (useful negative results)}

If the extracted geometric phase depends strongly on discretization artifacts,
or if $F_{\mu\nu}$ does not exhibit robust locality/propagation properties,
then the gauge interpretation should be rejected or confined to narrow regimes.
Likewise, if no stable localized modes exist without external scaffolding,
then the particle hypothesis fails for the chosen constitutive family.

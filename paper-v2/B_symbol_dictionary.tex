\section{Symbol dictionary}
\label{app:symbols}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.18\linewidth} p{0.76\linewidth}}
$\Omega\subset\R^3$ & Reference domain of the brane (3D coordinates $x=(x^1,x^2,x^3)$).\\
$\vecX(x,t)\in\R^4$ & Embedding map of the brane into ambient 4D Euclidean space.\\
$t$ & External evolution parameter (time).\\
$g_{ij}$ & Induced metric on the brane: $g_{ij}=\partial_i\vecX\cdot\partial_j\vecX$.\\
$\bar g_{ij}$ & Reference metric used to encode homogeneous pre-tension (estimator-level).\\
$\alpha$ & Reference-metric scale / prestretch parameter ($\bar g_{ij}=\alpha^2\delta_{ij}$).\\
$\rho_m$ & Mass density in the Lagrangian.\\
$W(g;\bar g)$ & Isotropic stored-energy density (hyperelastic).\\
$P_{Ai}$ & First Piola--Kirchhoff-like stress: $P_{Ai}=\partial W/\partial(\partial_i X^A)$.\\
$\vecu$ & Displacement field $\vecu=\vecX-\vecX_\star$.\\
$\bm{\epsilon}$ & Normalized polarization state of a narrowband multi-component wave.\\
$A_\mu$ & Berry (Pancharatnam--Berry) connection: $A_\mu=-i\bm{\epsilon}^\dagger\partial_\mu\bm{\epsilon}$.\\
$F_{\mu\nu}$ & Berry curvature: $F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu$.\\
$\mathcal{A}_\mu$ & Wilczek--Zee non-Abelian connection for a degenerate subspace.\\
$\Phi_\gamma$ & Holonomy / geometric phase along a closed loop $\gamma$: $\Phi_\gamma=\oint_\gamma A$.\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
